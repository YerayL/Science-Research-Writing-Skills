# Appendix A: Prefixes Used in Science Writing

## Core Idea
A reference list of common prefixes in scientific English, with meanings and examples. Mastering these helps decode unfamiliar terms and construct precise scientific vocabulary.

## Key Takeaway
Understanding these prefixes provides a systematic way to build and decode scientific vocabulary, especially useful for non-native English speakers.
